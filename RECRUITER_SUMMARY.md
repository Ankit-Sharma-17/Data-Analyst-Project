### 3. **Business Value Demonstrated**

#### Problem Solved
Telecom operators face ~30% annual churn and $1M+ in preventable revenue loss per 100K customers. This project demonstrates:

1. **Churn Quantification** - Identify that 31.6% of customers are at risk
2. **Root Cause Analysis** - Payment delays, support intensity, tenure drive churn
3. **Segment Prioritization** - "At-Risk" segment (45% of base, 42% churn rate) = highest intervention ROI
4. **Retention Economics** - $150K+ annual revenue recoverable through targeted retention

#### Stakeholder Value
- **CFO:** Revenue impact projection, risk quantification
- **CMO:** Segment profiles for targeted retention campaigns
- **VP Operations:** Operational metrics (support, payment health) and trends
- **CEO:** Executive dashboard, competitive position vs. churn benchmarks
